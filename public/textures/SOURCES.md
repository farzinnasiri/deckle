# Paper material

[paper-cotton.png](paper-cotton.png) was generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-09 for Deckle. It is a new material inspired by the user's paper references, not a crop of the reference app. CSS repeats it at 420px and multiplies it into the selected paper-stock tint.

Prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless repeating paper material for a writing app. Create a square high-resolution flat scan of clean uncoated fine cotton writing paper. Warm neutral near-white, very fine dense interwoven natural cellulose grain with softly mottled microscopic relief, a few extremely small tan fiber inclusions, quiet and tactile like real stationery. Texture fills the entire image, uniformly lit with no vignette or directional lighting, seamless edges. No sheet edges, text, objects, wrinkles, stains, large strands, scratches, patterns, obvious flecks or distressed vintage effects. The grain must remain subtle and legible underneath black printed text. This is a material albedo, not a scene.
