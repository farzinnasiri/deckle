# Deckle

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js, explicitly requested by Farzin. TypeScript and React.

## Purpose and users

A quiet writing instrument for people writing for themselves with a hardware keyboard. Success is a person spending longer writing than they intended, enjoying the act itself. DECKLE.md is the product authority.

Farzin's second-iteration instructions refine the brief: give the writing surface priority, offer an immersive view, physically feed the paper with sound as space fills, and allow selection and cursor navigation for earlier edits. Borrow analog sensations while preserving fast digital editing.

## Commitments

One walnut desk, one warm-grey machine, paper off a continuous roll, a physical tear to finish. No AI writing, accounts, nagging, or gamification. Every pasted passage is a distinct clipping. Text and replay logs stay local. Accessible labeled controls, reduced motion, silence, and larger type. New desks open ready to write; desk setup is optional. Backup, bulk Markdown export, and import live in the archive.

Writing allows edits anywhere and session undo/redo. The Draft/Ink switch has been removed; historical Ink marks and replay logs remain readable. Tearing files a piece; it never deletes it. The tray opens finished papers, and a visible save status communicates browser-local persistence. Immersive mode removes surrounding complexity and has a visible control to return to the desk.

## Web adaptations

Browser-local IndexedDB replaces native documents-folder storage. Markdown downloads and browser print-to-PDF provide portable exports. No promise of Force Touch, native filesystem access, or measured native audio latency. Automatic lighting uses local hours because exact sunset requires a location. No location permission on first run.

## Open decisions

Launch-quality recorded sound library, commissioned typeface, business model, and final product name remain undecided. Prototype assets must carry source and license information.
