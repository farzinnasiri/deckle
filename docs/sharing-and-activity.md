# Sharing and writing instruments

Share is available from the right-hand desk controls and the selected archived paper's menu. It renders a PNG locally; opening the preview does not upload writing to Deckle or a social network. Choose Portrait (4:5, 1080×1350), Square (1:1, 1080×1080), Story (9:16, 1080×1920), or Wide (16:9, 1200×675) using the visible ratio tiles.

Short writing is included in full. Longer writing uses an opening excerpt capped at 10, 8, 14, or 5 typeset rows, respectively. An ellipsis marks omitted content. This keeps text readable and leaves the saved paper unchanged. The paper's title, author stamp, stock, typeface and ink formatting appear in the image. The image is independent of the desk's lighting.

X opens its website composer directly with a bounded excerpt of the paper already filled in. The PNG downloads for manual attachment; browser composer links cannot attach a local image. Instagram's button is enabled on phones and iPads that can share PNG files, and opens the device's image share sheet so the user can choose Instagram. It is disabled on desktop. The separate generic share button uses the device share sheet when available, with a download fallback. Deckle does not publish anything automatically.

The text zoom controls range from 80 to 140 percent and affect both the preview and exported PNG. Excerpts use fewer rows and shorter lines at larger zoom so the writing remains inside the image. Reset returns to 100 percent; the original paper stays unchanged.

The machine has two counters for the current paper's body: nonblank written rows and characters, including spaces but excluding line breaks. Characters count user-perceived Unicode characters, so a combined emoji is one character. Titles are excluded.

The vertical Battery instrument sits on the left. Click it to slide out a metal tray with the existing daily written-row total and characters newly typed or pasted that day; click again, press Escape, or click outside to close it. Corrections and deletions do not subtract activity. This measures writing activity rather than the final paper's size. The character counter starts recording when this feature is introduced; older typing cannot be reconstructed reliably from imported papers.

The battery starts empty, gains 20 percentage points once on a day with non-whitespace writing, and loses 5 for every completed inactive day. The current day does not drain until it ends. Charge stays between 0 and 100. Calendar days use the browser's local timezone, independent of daylight-saving hour changes. Five illuminated cells show charge, with partial dimming within each 20-point cell. There are no rewards, notifications, or streak penalties.

Activity is stored with the account's local IndexedDB desk and included in its backup. It does not aggregate writing from multiple browsers. Cloud storage saves the full desk in Convex, including activity. Local storage stays in this browser.

The rasterizer is loaded only while sharing. The editor does not rasterize on keystrokes. Closing the dialog releases image URLs; rapid format changes discard obsolete results. Failed rendering offers Retry. Reduced-motion preferences disable counter and battery transitions.
