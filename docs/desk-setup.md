# Desk setup and layouts

Settings remains available in every layout. Choose **Customize desk → Layout** for Minimal, Balanced, or Full desk. Advanced starts from the selected preset and lets you show or hide individual formatting keys, paper controls, counters, lighting, sound, feed, saving, and desk shortcuts. Removing controls hides the mechanisms, without changing the paper or their saved values. The remaining controls pack together and move into their new positions. Older desks default to Full desk. Layout choices are stored with the existing local and Convex desk settings and included in backups.

The settings inspector focuses the actual desk surface it edits: paper, machine, lamp, rail, or desk. Paper changes are immediately visible on the sheet. The Light section keeps the actual shade, pull cord, rail slider interactive, while writing and filing stay disabled.

Settings → Author → Guided setup opens the optional seven-step tour. Its panel sits alongside the working preview, or below it on narrow screens. Paper selection focuses the sheet; the lighting lesson points to the working shade and rail slider and moves keyboard focus to the chosen control. Desk and layout choices rearrange the preview. Save desk settings keeps the choices; Close setup or Escape restores the desk as it was before the tour. Keyboard navigation includes the demonstrated desk controls. Reduced motion removes camera zooms and animated reflow.

The Battery instrument is collapsed by default on the left, below the room-light switch. Its five vertical cells show charge. Clicking it slides a metal counter tray out from behind the fixed battery to show daily lines and characters, and clicking outside or pressing Escape closes it. Hover or keyboard focus explains charging and draining in two lines; on mobile, tapping to open the counters also shows that help. Its charge rules and local storage remain unchanged.

The type-size keys have four detents: Small (16px), Standard (18px), Large (22px), and Extra large (26px). Four red pilot lamps show the level, and the keys stop at either end. Desks using the earlier two-size preference retain Standard or Large; the new level is saved with the existing local and Convex settings.

Opening settings moves and scales the entire desk preview uniformly, preserving its controls and proportions. The lamp follows that movement. Focus mode retracts the cord and side controls, lowers the machine body, and enlarges the remaining paper and rail by 12 percent; the focus control remains available to return. Reduced motion switches these positions without travel.

As the sheet feeds beyond the desk's visible height, it rolls backward over a rounded crest, the way paper keeps the platen's set. The torn edge goes behind the bend, the fibres foreshorten, and the writing turns away from the reader; the top of the bend is the brightest part of the sheet. Rolling back relaxes the crest and reveals earlier writing on the live sheet. The crest is visual only: the document, selection, backup, and cloud content are unchanged.

The account row displays the existing identity and avatar with a single sign-out action. It does not expose Clerk profile editing, email additions, connected accounts, image uploads, or account switching.

The wall switch controls room illumination independently of the top and rail lamps. Entering the Light section darkens the room and turns on any lamp that was off, then leaves the switch and independent brightness and colour controls available. There are no room-light preset choices or combined scene presets. The surface picker has Walnut, Oak, Leather, Linen, Slate, Cherry, Maple, and Rosewood.


## Finite papers and copying

A paper has 120 logical body lines. Each explicit Return starts a line, including empty and trailing lines; visual wrapping does not use this allowance. The machine counter includes those lines and starts at one on an empty sheet. At capacity, another Return or an oversized paste is rejected as a whole and prompts filing the paper for a fresh sheet. Existing longer papers remain intact, and removing lines or editing within their current line count stays possible. Archive imports and replay do not truncate saved writing.

The Copy paper control copies the complete title and body as rich HTML and plain text. Headings, bold, italic, underline, crossed-out text, quotes and ink colors are preserved where the destination supports them. Clipboard HTML escapes the writing rather than interpreting it as markup. Browsers without rich clipboard support use plain text and report that fallback.
